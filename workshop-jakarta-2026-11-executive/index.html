Questions

What do I need to prepare?

Your own laptop with an AI agent installed — OpenCode or Claude Code. This is a prerequisite for the hands-on sessions. Wi-Fi is provided at the venue. You will receive a step-by-step setup guide after registering, and mentors help with any remaining setup from 09:30.

Do I need technical or programming knowledge?

No. The workshop is built for leaders and managers. You work with agents in plain language; the Spring AI agent is shown as a live demo, not built by you.

Do I need an API key or a paid AI subscription?

No. Everything runs over the internet with free models: OpenCode brings its own free models (OpenCode Zen), and NVIDIA offers free models at build.nvidia.com — you only need a free account. If you have Claude Code, you can use Claude models such as Opus.

Which language is the workshop in?

The workshop is held entirely in Bahasa Indonesia. This website is available in both languages.

Why only 30 seats?

So that every participant gets personal attention and room to discuss their own organisation. With the speaker and three mentors, the ratio is about one mentor per ten participants.

Yes. Register each participant via the form and mention your company — payment instructions and an invoice follow by WhatsApp or email. For several seats from one company, chat with Mova on WhatsApp.
