What You Will Learn

Five sessions, one goal: understand the theory behind LLMs and AI agents, see it work in hands-on examples — and know what it means for your studies, research and career.

AI or DIE — why now

Disrupted, irrelevant, extinct — or AI-native. The TOE framework (technology, organisation, environment) and the path from digitalisation to agentic AI.

How an LLM works

Tokens, embeddings, transformers and attention, pre-training, fine-tuning and RLHF — why an LLM is a “clever parrot” and why it hallucinates.

Anatomy of an agent

LLM as brain, memory, RAG, tools and MCP — and what makes AI agentic: autonomy, multi-step planning and revision.

Hands-on with AI agents

OpenCode and Claude Code for spec-driven development, and an agent that researches and summarises sources for you.

Security, ethics & integrity

An agent can do everything you are allowed to do. Sandboxes, secrets, prompt injection, data protection (UU PDP) — and academic integrity.

Inside a Java agent & your career

A code walk-through of “Warung Digital” with Spring Boot and Spring AI — and the “product builder” role you are studying for.

Questions

What do I need to prepare?

Your own laptop with an AI agent installed — OpenCode or Claude Code. This is a prerequisite for the hands-on examples. To run the Spring AI example yourself you also need JDK 21, Maven and Git; otherwise you can follow it on screen. Wi-Fi is provided at the venue. You will receive the setup guide after registering.

Do I need to know how to program?

No. The focus is on theory, and in the hands-on examples the agent does the coding. Basic programming knowledge helps to follow the Spring AI code walk-through in Session 5.

Do I need an API key or a paid AI subscription?

No. Everything runs over the internet with free models: OpenCode brings its own free models (OpenCode Zen), and NVIDIA offers free models at build.nvidia.com — you only need a free account. If you have Claude Code, you can use Claude models such as Opus.

Which language is the workshop in?

The workshop is held entirely in Bahasa Indonesia. This website is available in both languages.

How does hands-on work with 100 participants?

Every example is prepared step by step in the workshop repository, so you can follow along at your own pace. The speaker and five mentors walk the room — about one mentor per twenty participants.

How is this different from the full-day workshop?

The full-day workshop (IDR 100.000, 50 seats) has long hands-on labs in which you build your own agent with Spring AI. This academic edition takes three hours, puts the theory first and uses shorter hands-on examples.
